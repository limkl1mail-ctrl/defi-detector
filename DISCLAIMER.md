# Disclaimer

## Not investment advice

Nothing published in this repository is investment advice, financial advice, legal advice, or a solicitation to buy or sell any asset. Readers are responsible for their own due diligence and decisions.

## Research nature

These are adversarial due-diligence reports. By design, the methodology assumes every project is guilty until proven innocent. A report's tone is therefore skeptical, even when the underlying findings are mixed or favorable. Read the full report — not just the verdict — before drawing conclusions.

## Sources and verification

Reports are based on publicly available information as of the date stamped in each report's filename. Sources include block explorers, DeFiLlama, project documentation, audit reports, independent analyst commentary, and journalism. Where a claim could not be independently verified, the report labels it as *unverified*, *claimed*, or *inferred*. Where verified, the report labels it as *verified* and cites the primary source.

## Time sensitivity

Each report is a snapshot. Protocols change: contracts get upgraded, multisigs get re-keyed, audits get published, tokens unlock, hacks happen. A report's Monitor section lists the specific triggers that would invalidate its findings. **Reports are not maintained after publication.** Always check the report date and the current state of the protocol before acting on any finding.

## AI-assisted research

Reports are produced by [Claude](https://claude.com/claude-code) (an AI assistant by Anthropic) operating against the methodology in [`METHODOLOGY.md`](./METHODOLOGY.md). The methodology is deterministic about epistemology (source hierarchy, severity tiers, required sections) but the underlying language model is probabilistic. Errors, omissions, and misreadings are possible. Verify critical findings against the cited primary sources before relying on them.

## Conflict of interest

## Corrections

If you are a project team or third party and believe a report contains a factual error, open an issue on this repository with the specific claim, the report it appears in, and primary-source evidence supporting the correction. Corrections will be appended to the relevant report as a dated addendum; the original text will not be silently edited.
